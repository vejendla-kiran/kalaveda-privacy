KalaVeda Privacy Policy

Applies to the KalaVeda panchang app for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac. Effective 24 November 2026.

In short: KalaVeda works entirely on your device. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. Your location never leaves your device.

What the app uses, and why

  • Location (approximate or precise). Used only to calculate sunrise, sunset, tithi, nakshatra, kalam timings and the place names in the sankalpam for where you are. The calculation happens on your device.
  • Location in the background (optional). If you allow it, the home-screen and lock-screen widgets use your current location so their timings stay correct when you travel, even while the app is closed. You can turn this off at any time in your device settings; the widget then uses the place from when you last opened the app.
  • Start at boot (Android). Lets the widget schedule its next update after the device restarts.
  • Details you type in. Your name, gotra, deity and similar details for the sankalpam, and your settings (calendar tradition, language, colours), are saved only on your device.

What we do not do

  • KalaVeda has no account, no sign-in and no server. The Android app does not even request permission to use the internet.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting libraries are included.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data from app stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store may provide the developer with aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as install counts and crash reports) under their own privacy policies. These do not include your location or anything you type into the app.

Bundled reference data

Place names come from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and rivers, mountain ranges and seas from Natural Earth (public domain). This data ships inside the app and is searched on your device; looking up your place sends nothing anywhere.

Deleting your data

All data is on your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it completely. There is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

Children

KalaVeda is a general-audience reference app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
