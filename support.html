KalaVeda Support

Offline Hindu panchang for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/vejendla-kiran/kalaveda-privacy/issues. Please include your calendar tradition, the date and the city, but not your exact location.

The timings look wrong for my city

KalaVeda calculates everything for the place your device reports. Open the app, tap Refresh on the location card, and check that the city shown is correct. Sunrise is calculated for sea level, so it can differ by a minute or two in hills.

The widget did not update after I travelled

Allow location "Always" (iPhone) or "Allow all the time" (Android) for KalaVeda. Otherwise the widget keeps using the place from when you last opened the app; opening the app once at the new place fixes it.

When does the day name change?

Traditionally at sunrise. You can choose sunrise, sunset or midnight in Settings → Day changes at.

Privacy

KalaVeda works offline and never sends your location or anything you type anywhere. See the privacy policy.
